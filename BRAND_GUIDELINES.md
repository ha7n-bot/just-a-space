# JUST A SPACE / visual identity

## Evidence and scope

The research register links the Al Nakheel branch to the English name JUST A SPACE. The project has no owner-issued logo master, identity manual, licensed photography, signage specification, or approved marketing slogan. Use the brand name as ordinary bold text until the owner supplies and licenses the official asset. The Arabic transcription and campaign copy require owner approval before launch.

## Editorial direction

A warm paper background and a dark green action color frame a bespoke still life. In each language the wide desktop hero pairs a short headline with a larger editorial image. Arabic uses its natural script direction and Latin type has an italic second line. No photographic content is mirrored.

The CMS gallery captures image creator, dimensions, permissions, a source, a verification date and bilingual alternatives.
