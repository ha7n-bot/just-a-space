## Search coverage and interpretation

Arabic and English searches included several spellings of the café, its district, handle, signature-product candidates, official menu, QR/POS links and delivery providers including The Chefz, HungerStation, Jahez, Keeta and Ninja. Results returned differently named restaurants and unmatched outlets. Their prices were excluded. No branch-authorized ordering page or dated first-party product list was identified. The supplied Instagram and short Maps URLs did not produce a readable first-party response. Search-index material from Restaurant Guru was available although the supplied menu page was inaccessible. No paywall, login restriction or Maps API control was bypassed.

Corner's outbound Directions query contains the Place ID recorded in the JSON. World Coffee Map links the same pin via Google's feature identifier. The two Google destination pages returned a JavaScript notice in this environment. The research confirms a candidate identifier, not an authenticated Place Details result. Directory repeat publication can reflect common upstream data.

## Brand and competitive context

Daymark and World Coffee Map associate the café with a restrained architectural interior. These descriptions favour generous margins, deliberate contrast and letting approved photographs carry the gallery. None of the retrieved evidence establishes an official logo file, typeface, palette, camera viewpoint, packaging system, slogan or image licence. Use textual identification and explicitly labelled concept work for review. Confirm official signs, packaging and photography against an owner-supplied reference pack before production.

Brew92's Riyadh branch groups its address, day-specific schedules and directions in one visit flow. The operational insight is that visitors comparing hours and transit need these together. No competitor text, photography, products or recipes were reused. Customers' product mentions helped prioritize menu verification of iced drinks, coffee and desserts. These observations do not substantiate ongoing availability or café quality claims.

## Legal and editorial checks

Use a price approved for this outlet, sales channel, serving and tax treatment. ZATCA section 5.1 describes inclusive displayed prices in the local retail market. The operator must confirm registration and the final amount. SFDA covers menu disclosures including caffeine, high salt and activity equivalents. Have the operator or a qualified reviewer determine the required disclosures, verify underlying values and approve how they appear in both languages before activating a public menu. Never calculate a nutritional claim from a product name.

Before processing customer data, identify the actual operator, contact for data-subject requests, purposes, legal bases, recipients, processor locations and retention/deletion schedule. The PDPL includes information, access/copy, correction and destruction rights subject to the law's conditions. The official Arabic text and implementing regulations govern. Scope and hosting details require review by the controller. A link to another website, authentication cookies, access logs and contact submissions have different privacy consequences. Document the deployed configuration.
